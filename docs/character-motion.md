# Scroll character motion

Asset: `public/media/developer-motion-atlas.png`.

The built-in `image_gen.imagegen` created a transparent 4 × 3 atlas with 12 character poses, referencing the approved cutout plus typing and presenting frames extracted from the supplied video. These are AI-assisted reference-based poses, not pixel-exact video frame crops.

The scene samples one atlas cell at a time. Scroll position selects frames 0–11; reverse scrolling selects the same frames in reverse. There is no clock-driven playback. Reduced-motion mode uses neutral frame 1. Frame borders have a half-pixel UV inset and mipmaps are disabled to prevent adjacent frames bleeding into the sprite.

## Exact generation prompt

Create ONE transparent character animation SPRITE SHEET, exactly 4 columns by 3 rows = 12 equal square cells, 2048 x 1536 pixels if possible. Inputs: image 1 is the approved character cutout (identity and exact clay materials); image 2 is the source typing frame; image 3 is the source presenting frame. Preserve the approved character's sculpted spiky dark hair, friendly large brown eyes, precise beard and moustache, tan skin, olive sweater and warm clay lighting. Each cell contains ONLY the same waist-up character, no desk, chair, computer, keyboard, plants, background, labels or borders. Use ACTUAL alpha transparency. All 12 cells must have identical camera, character scale, torso position, head size, hair-top height and waist baseline. Head centered at x=45% within each cell, top of hair at y=8%, waist baseline at y=94%, ample space right for the hand. No feet or legs. Read left-to-right then top-to-bottom as consecutive animation poses: 1 looking down, both hands resting in a typing position; 2 eyes lift toward viewer, hands remain down; 3 looking at viewer with smile, right hand beginning to lift; 4 right forearm halfway raised; 5 open right hand next to face starting a wave; 6 raised hand waving slightly outward; 7 same hand waving slightly inward and a subtle blink; 8 hand lowers partway, face begins turning to right; 9 face turns about 25 degrees toward right, hand beginning a pointing gesture; 10 right index finger points to the right toward imaginary project cards; 11 slightly stronger pointing and smiling at the project; 12 relaxed presenting pose with right hand open palm towards the right. Continuous small changes between adjacent frames. Preserve identity across all cells; never draw different characters or change the shirt or hair. This is a production animation atlas, uniform grid is mandatory, no merged cells, no character crossing a cell boundary, no checkerboard painted into the image.
