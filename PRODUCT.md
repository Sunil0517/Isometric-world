# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, prospective collaborators, and visitors exploring Sunil Kumawat’s development work. Visitors can explore a playable isometric world or read the same portfolio as standard HTML.

## Product Purpose

Present Sunil’s resume accurately through an interactive Hidden Leaf Village portfolio, with accessible paths to professional history, skills, education, certifications, and contact details.

## Brand Personality

Playful, adventurous, thoughtful. The requested reference is Hidden Leaf Village: a forest settlement with shinobi training grounds, tiled rooftops and lanterns. The Hokage monument wall is intentionally removed.

## Anti-references

The repository explicitly avoids invented employment, achievements, proficiency scores, and unconfigured contact links presented as real. Generic sample projects must give way to resume-supported work.

## Design Principles

- Use the supplied resume as the source of professional facts.
- Let the village invite exploration while keeping professional information direct.
- Keep every portfolio section available without walking or game rewards.
- Preserve the standard HTML route for visitors who prefer reading.
- Preserve keyboard, mobile, focus management, and reduced-motion support.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain the existing accessible dialogs, keyboard navigation, touch controls, muted-by-default audio, reduced decorative motion setting, and server-rendered portfolio without WebGL.
